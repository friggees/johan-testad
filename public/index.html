03 / BEHIND THE MUSIC

Between nature
and noise.

I’m Johan. A composer drawn to the tension between raw landscapes and industrial textures.

Born in Solna and now based in the village of Tibble, Dalarna, I grew up with art and music in many forms. That contrast still shapes the way I write: intimate melodies meeting unexpected sounds.

From Goliat to Beartown, my work follows the emotional life of a story. Always searching for the music that belongs to it.
